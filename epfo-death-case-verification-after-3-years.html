OFFICE NOTE

Subject: Verification through Enforcement Officer in delayed death claim case – reg.

A Composite Claim Form in Death Cases has been received in this office in respect of deceased member Sh/Smt , PF Account No. .

As per the claim form, the date of death of the member is . The death claim has been received in this office after a period of more than three years from the date of death. As per the applicable instructions/procedure, death claims received after three years of the death of the member require verification by the Enforcement Officer (EO) before further processing of the claim.

The case may, therefore, be forwarded to the Compliance Wing with a request to kindly depute an Enforcement Officer to verify the genuineness of the death claim, particulars of the deceased member, claimant(s), and other relevant facts/documents, and submit a detailed verification report.

In view of the pendency of the death claim, it is requested that the Enforcement Officer may kindly be deputed and the verification report be furnished within 03 days of receipt of this note, so that the claim may be processed further as per rules.

Submitted to Compliance Wing for necessary action, please.
